⚠️ Engineering Challenge

Moving Beyond Traditional Remote Controls

Why standard cloud APIs and traditional Bluetooth fall short for physical robotics

✕ Traditional & Cloud Approaches

  • Cloud Latency (1–2s): Cloud-based speech APIs introduce unacceptable lag; a 2s delay means your robot collides before halting.
  • macOS BLE Virtual Port Trap: macOS does not assign virtual serial ports to BLE 5.0 peripherals like Keyestudio BT24.
  • Runaway Motor Danger: Dropped packets or interrupted connections leave DC motors spinning unchecked indefinitely.

✓ CyberRover AI Solution

  • 100% Offline Inference: Local CPU neural models provide sub-50ms instant response with zero cloud dependency.
  • Native CoreBluetooth GATT: Direct BLE GATT service (0xFFE0 / 0xFFE1) communication via Python Bleak.
  • Deterministic Fail-Safe: Timed auto-stops on voice commands + Arduino 500ms safety watchdog.
⏱️ Slide 2: The Challenge (Target: 0:20 - 0:45 | 25 seconds)

"Traditional RC vehicles rely on handheld controllers. When engineers try to build voice or vision controllers, they often struggle with cloud latency — where a 2-second cloud delay means your robot hits a wall.

Furthermore, modern BLE modules don't present standard serial COM ports on macOS. Our engineering goal was to build a low-latency, 100% local pipeline with native Bluetooth GATT and robust hardware fail-safes."
👁️ Computer Vision

Mode 1: Hand Gesture Steering

Real-time tracking of 21 3D landmarks at 30+ FPS via MediaPipe

📐 Vector Tilt Steering

Acts as a virtual steering wheel:

Computes continuous wrist-to-knuckle angle via math.atan2. Tilting hand past 40° smoothly steers Left or Right.

✋ Orientation Awareness

Directional distinction:

Palm normal 3D cross-product distinguishes Back of Hand (Forward drive) from Open Palm (Reverse drive).

⚡ Snappy Safety Stop

Closed fist trigger:

Closing your hand into a fist triggers an immediate emergency stop in just 2 video frames (~60ms).

Intuitive & Rotation-Independent: Uses Euclidean landmark distances rather than screen coordinates, ensuring reliable finger detection regardless of hand angle.

🎙️ Speech Recognition

Mode 2: Real-Time Offline Voice Control

Constrained Vosk/Kaldi grammar engine with < 50ms latency & timed auto-stops

"FORWARD" Straight Drive 1.0s Timer
"BACK" Reverse Drive 1.0s Timer
"STEP" Precision Micro-Nudge 0.5s Timer
"LEFT" / "RIGHT" Differential Turn 0.5s Timer
"STOP" Emergency Brake Immediate (<10ms)

Deterministic Safety Design

Replaced dangerous continuous driving with discrete, deterministic movements:

  • Zero Cloud / 100% Private: Audio is processed strictly in-memory on the Mac CPU.
  • Grammar-Filtered: False positives like "reverse" or background talk are filtered at the decoder level.
  • Auto-Stop Guarantee: Every directional command halts automatically after its duration expires.
⏱️ Slide 4: Voice Mode (Target: 1:10 - 1:35 | 25 seconds)

"In Voice Mode, we integrated Vosk. By locking down our vocabulary to a strict grammar of 6 action words, recognition takes less than 50 milliseconds.

Crucially, we abandoned dangerous continuous movement in favor of timed increments: saying 'Left' or 'Right' rotates for exactly half a second and auto-stops. Saying 'Step' nudges forward for precision parking. You never risk a runaway robot."
